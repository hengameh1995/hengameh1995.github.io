The Problem

Why This
Matters

Kids now grow up with AI that can paint finished pictures from a few words, so many skip the brushstrokes and start from an AI image.

Painting trains hands, builds creativity and pride. The risk is the quality gap: next to a flawless AI picture, a child feels disappointed or just copies the machine.

Research Question
How can we design an AI interface for children's painting that does not harm their creativity — but instead helps them discover and develop their own personal style?
  • 01Help children with AI without disappointment or dependence on copying.
  • 02Build a simple, safe AI painting tool — working name Peekaboo, shipped as Picabon — that encourages a child's own style.
  • 03Suggest future apps and toys that use AI positively for children's art and learning.
State of the Art

What Painting
Is Actually For

Pablo Picasso spoke often about the value of children's painting and its role in real art.

"It took me four years to paint like Raphael, but a lifetime to paint like a child."Pablo Picasso

Picasso learned realistic painting in a few years, then spent a lifetime chasing a child's freedom. Lowenfeld's stages of artistic development show that early free painting builds imagination and problem-solving, but it fades fast: Land and Jarman found "genius-level" divergent thinking falls from 98% in young children to far lower by the teens.

Pablo Picasso

Three existing tools were researched directly, and each — in its own way — nudges kids toward copying rather than creating.

Drawing Robot for Kids

Talks, uses 16 colours, draws alongside the child from 100 learning cards.

+ Cheap, hands-on, good for beginners.

− Kids stick to copying samples over time.

Doodle AI

Transforms a child's drawing into a high-quality image online, no age limit.

+ Sparks imagination with a "better" version.

− Disappoints with results beyond real skill; teaches nothing.

T3 Smart Drawing Projector

Projects vectorised drawings onto a surface for easy redrawing.

+ Builds basic hand skills early on.

− Reduces imagination — kids only trace, never invent.

Picabon sits between copying and perfection: no fixed samples, no perfect result, only small, encouraging steps while the child leads.

The Concept

A Loop,
Not a Mirror

The child paints, Picabon reads the painting and turns it into words, and an AI storyteller writes a short, positive tale with characters the child loves — never a picture to copy.

It then gives one spoken suggestion, like “Maybe add bigger wings for your dragon?” Everything stays verbal.

Character Design

From Troll
to Yarn

Knitted, On Purpose
Yarn can be shaped into almost anything — and it reads as cute, warm, and handmade before it says a single word.

The first prototype used Shrek and was scrapped: he is copyrighted. The direction shifted to something neutral and original.

Inspired by Buddy, a children's app with an expressive 3D lead, the character moved from 2D to 3D.

With limited time, the finalized character design was illustrated in 2D and animated in After Effects. For Mia, a five-year-old who loves Peppa Pig, a knitted 3D avatar inspired by Peppa was sculpted in Blender.

Voice & Language

The System
Prompt

The prompt behind story generation: it takes the painting's elements plus retrieved story chunks and asks for one short, positive story.

System Prompt
You are a warm, fun, and creative storyteller for children aged 5-10. The child drew a painting. Here are the main elements extracted from the painting: {painting_elements} Here are some story pieces from famous children's books that are similar: {retrieved_chunks} Create a short, exciting, and positive 4-6 sentence story for the child. - Mix the painting elements with ideas from the similar books. - Make the child feel proud of their painting. - Use simple, happy, and magical language. - End with something nice.

The Prototype

Core Experience
A working prototype takes a painting, extracts its elements, retrieves story chunks, and returns a short spoken story plus one gentle suggestion, delivered by the knitted character.
Seven Components
  • Painting input & feature extraction
  • Textual description generator
  • Story retrieval engine (semantic search)
  • Story & feedback generator (LLM + system prompt)
  • Character database + voice
  • Text-to-speech + animation trigger
  • UI / character display layer, in Figma
Design Constraints
A very simple interface for young readers, safety designed in from the start, and robustness for any drawing style, from scribble to careful sketch.

Reflection

What Actually
Happened

In testing, mixing drawing and storytelling engaged children: kids aged 6–10 tried more new colours and shapes after story-based suggestions than after copy-based tools, a sign Picabon builds confidence and personal style.

Against the research question: with verbal stories, no visual models and no copying, AI can support a child's style when it is built around play, not perfection.

Strengths

  • Child-led: verbal-only feedback, no visual model
  • Personalised through favourite characters + retrieval
  • Combines drawing and storytelling
  • Ethical: no disappointment from “perfect” images
  • Scalable: database + prompt engineering

Weaknesses

  • Still a prototype: image understanding not fully automated
  • 20K-chunk story database causes some repetition
  • Only informal sessions, no large user study
  • 2D animation feels less alive than planned 3D
  • Currently English-only

Conclusion

What This Proves,
What's Still Open

Picabon delivers an AI storytelling app that responds to a child's own drawing and preserves their visual expression. Children retell the story to parents or friends, building narrative thinking and confidence — AI as a creative partner, not a replacement.

Still open: an incomplete story database limits variety, very abstract drawings are hard to interpret, and full 3D animation didn't fit the time. Age adaptability, more languages and parental controls need more work.

  • →Multiplayer storytelling between kids, not just child and character.
  • →Parental controls and progress tracking.
  • →Better drawing interpretation with a multimodal model (GPT-4o, LLaVA).
