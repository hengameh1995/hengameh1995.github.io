Artist by nature.
Designer by craft.

I am Hengameh Koliyaei — an artist who came to design through fine art. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts and a Master's degree in Human-Computer Interaction, which means I approach every project with both a painterly eye and a deep understanding of how people think, feel, and navigate digital spaces.

My work lives at the crossroads of graphic design and UI/UX — translating complex ideas into visual experiences that are not only beautiful but genuinely usable. I believe the best interfaces are the ones that feel like they were made by someone who truly understands both art and the human mind.
